Instructions and Overview

This is the 2016 remake of Raft Wars. Raft Wars is a level-based shooting game created by Martijn Kunst, where the player control two characters, Simon and his brother, who will need to defend their treasure from different kinds of enemies. Initially armed with their raft and only some tennis balls, rockets and grenades they'll need to focus on their aim and power in order to defeat Vikings, pirates, gangs, and angry neighbors.
